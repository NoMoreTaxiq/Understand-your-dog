Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how Andrew — The Dog Trainer collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, contact me, book training, or use my services.

Who I am

I am Andrew Heaney, trading as Andrew — The Dog Trainer.

Information I may collect

Depending on how you use my services, I may receive:

  • your name and contact details;
  • information you provide when making an enquiry or booking;
  • information about your dog and its behaviour that you choose to tell me;
  • photos or videos of your dog, including videos you send to me for remote behaviour analysis; and
  • technical information about your visit to this website, where applicable through cookies or similar technologies.

How I use your information

I use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and communicate with you;
  • arrange and provide dog-training services;
  • review videos and other information you voluntarily send for remote training;
  • manage appointments and bookings;
  • take payments and maintain appropriate business records;
  • understand how people use my website and measure advertising where applicable; and
  • protect the website, my business and customers from misuse or fraud.

Lawful bases

I process information where necessary to take steps at your request before providing a service, to perform a contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, or where I have a legitimate interest in operating and improving my business. Where the law requires consent, I will ask for it.

Who I share information with

I only share personal information where necessary for the purposes described above or where the law requires it. Depending on how you use the service, this may include service providers such as:

  • WhatsApp / Meta, when you choose to contact me or send material through WhatsApp;
  • Square, when you use the online appointment-booking service;
  • Meta, where advertising measurement tools such as the Meta Pixel are used and where the required consent has been provided; and
  • professional advisers, payment providers, or public authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required.

Videos and dog-training information

If you use my remote service, you may send me videos or other information about your dog. Please avoid including unnecessary information about other people in videos. If other people are clearly identifiable, you should make sure you have the appropriate permission to share the recording with me.

I use information you provide to analyse the behaviour shown and prepare your training advice. I do not sell your videos or personal information.

How long I keep information

I keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing the service, dealing with queries, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods can therefore vary depending on the type of information.

Cookies and website measurement

This website may use cookies or similar technologies. In particular, advertising measurement technology such as the Meta Pixel may be used to understand visits and measure advertising performance. Non-essential cookies or similar technologies will only be used where the required consent has been obtained.

Some third-party services linked from this website, such as Square, WhatsApp, Facebook, Amazon and external media or font services, have their own privacy and cookie policies. When you leave this website and use one of those services, their terms and policies apply.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you have rights under UK data protection law including the right to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing, and data portability where applicable. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent.

To exercise your rights or ask a privacy question, contact me using the details above.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your personal information, please contact me first so I can try to resolve the issue. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to my services, the website, or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
