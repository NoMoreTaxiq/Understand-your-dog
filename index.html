The session

I'll train you to train your dog.

The biggest change usually happens when the humans understand what the dog needs from them. I don't just take the lead, make your dog behave and hand them back.

I explain what I'm seeing, demonstrate what to do, then coach you through doing it yourself. We can cover behaviour, boundaries, engagement, lead work, recall, training around the home, mental stimulation and nutrition where relevant.

The goal is that you finish the session knowing what to do tomorrow morning — without me standing beside you.

Before you book

Questions people usually ask me.

Can you help with my dog's specific problem?

Probably. I've worked with pulling, reactivity, recall, resource guarding, aggression, barking, separation issues, puppies and general household behaviour. Tell me what's happening and I'll be straight with you about whether I can help.

Will one session really be enough?

For many owners, one intensive session gives them the understanding and tools they need to continue the work themselves. Every dog is different, so I won't pretend every issue disappears in two hours — but you should leave knowing what needs to change and how to work on it.

What does the guarantee mean?

I expect you to see a significant improvement when you consistently follow the methods we work on together. If you don't see that improvement within 7 days, you can ask for your money back.

Where do sessions take place?

1-to-1 sessions are carried out with you and your dog, usually at your home and in the environments where the behaviour actually matters. I'm based in Warrington and work across the surrounding North West area.

Do you offer remote help?

Yes. If you're outside my travelling area, use the Video Assessment or Online Consultation options at the top of the site.
