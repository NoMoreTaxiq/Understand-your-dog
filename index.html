What kind of behaviour can I send?

Anything you can safely capture that you're struggling to understand — for example barking, pulling, reacting, hiding, jumping or other unwanted or confusing behaviour.

Do I need to know what's causing it?

No. That's part of the point. Give me the context you know and let the footage show me the rest.

Is this an in-person service?

No. It's a remote service, so you can send footage from wherever you are.

What if the behaviour is complicated?

If the footage suggests that a situation needs a more involved assessment or specialist support, I'll tell you rather than pretending a short video can answer everything.
