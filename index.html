Questions

Before you book

Do I upload the video when I book?

No. The website is for booking and payment. Once your assessment is booked, I'll send you clear instructions for sending the footage.

Do I need a professional camera?

No. Your phone is absolutely fine. The important thing is being able to see the behaviour clearly.

What happens after I pay?

You'll receive the next-step instructions for sending your video and the essential information I need. I don't want you filling out a huge questionnaire before you've even started.

Can you help with something that isn't listed?

Yes. The examples above are common problems, not a closed list. If you can show me the behaviour, I can assess what I'm seeing.

Is this available outside the UK?

Yes. The remote assessment is designed to work from anywhere in the world.

What if I need more support afterwards?

If the behaviour calls for further support, I'll tell you what I think would be useful rather than pretending one assessment solves everything.
