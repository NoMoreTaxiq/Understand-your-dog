WHY VIDEO?

Descriptions can miss the details.

A dog may bark, lunge, hide, pull, freeze or react in a way that's difficult to explain afterwards. Small details in posture, movement, environment and what happens immediately before or after can change how the behaviour is understood.

Video gives me something far more useful than a description: the opportunity to watch the behaviour for myself.
