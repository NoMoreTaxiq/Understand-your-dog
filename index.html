How I train

I train you to train your dog.

Two hours with me isn't supposed to magically replace what happens at home. The difference is that I show you what to do, why you're doing it, and how to turn ordinary moments into training opportunities.

We'll work with your dog in the environment where the behaviour actually happens. You leave the session understanding your dog better and knowing what to practise next.

Most people only need one intensive session, providing they follow the methods afterwards.

Questions

Before you book.

Will one session really be enough?

For many owners, yes. The aim of the intensive is to identify what's going wrong, start making meaningful changes and teach you how to continue. Your results afterwards depend on consistently putting the methods into practice.

Other trainers haven't helped. Why will this be different?

I focus heavily on what is happening between you and your dog in everyday life, rather than simply teaching exercises for an hour. Have a look through the customer results above: many owners came to me after struggling for some time or trying other approaches.

What happens if I don't see an improvement?

Follow the methods we work through together. If you don't see a significant improvement within seven days, you can ask for your money back.

Do you only work in Warrington?

I'm based in Warrington and cover the wider North West. Send me your postcode before booking if you're unsure whether I cover your area.

Do you offer remote help?

Yes. The Video Behaviour Assessment and Online Consultation remain available through the links at the top of the site.
