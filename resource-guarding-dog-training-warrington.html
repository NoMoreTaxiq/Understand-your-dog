Resource guarding dog training in Warrington

If your dog freezes, growls, snaps or becomes defensive around food, toys, stolen objects, beds or people, the first job is to understand exactly what is being guarded and what happens around it.

Don't turn every guarded item into a confrontation.

Resource guarding needs sensible management as well as training. Repeatedly forcing objects away or testing whether the dog will react can make the situation more difficult and can create unnecessary risk.

What I'll look at

We'll look at the resources involved, warning signals, household routines, how people currently respond, whether the dog is stealing items to trigger interaction and what changes can reduce conflict while training is put in place.

A practical plan for home

The session is designed to leave you with clear management and training steps you can continue. Where there is a bite risk, safety and preventing rehearsals of the behaviour come first.
