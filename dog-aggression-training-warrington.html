Dog aggression training in Warrington

Growling, lunging, snapping or aggressive behaviour can make everyday life stressful. I work with you and your dog in the environment where the behaviour actually happens, then give you a practical plan to continue after I leave.

We deal with the behaviour in front of us.

Aggression is not one single problem with one standard fix. I look at what triggers the behaviour, what happens immediately before it, how you respond, and what the dog has learned from those situations. Then we work on the changes that matter to your dog and your household.

What we can work on

Reactions towards dogs or people, growling and snapping, guarding spaces or possessions, difficult behaviour around visitors, lead-related aggression and situations where you no longer feel confident handling your dog.

One focused session. £99.

Your 1-to-1 intensive session lasts up to two hours and takes place at your home and/or in your local environment. The aim is not to keep you buying endless sessions. It is to make progress and leave you knowing what to do next.
