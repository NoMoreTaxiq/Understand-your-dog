Dog pulling on the lead?

A walk shouldn't feel like a wrestling match. If your dog drags you, surges ahead, lunges or switches off as soon as the lead goes on, we'll work on the whole picture — not just tell you to keep stopping every few metres.

“Within 10 minutes he was walking to heel.”

Why dogs keep pulling

Pulling is often reinforced because it works: the dog gets to the smell, dog, person or destination. Excitement before leaving home, inconsistent lead handling and lack of attention can all add to it. I look at what your dog is actually doing and what is maintaining the behaviour.

What you'll learn

We'll work on practical lead handling, attention, pace, boundaries and how to stop the walk beginning at a level of excitement where your dog is already difficult to reach.
