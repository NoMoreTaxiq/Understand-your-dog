Dog recall training in Warrington

If “come” works in the kitchen but disappears the second your dog sees another dog, catches a scent or finds something more interesting, we need to make recall matter outside the house too.

Recall is more than shouting louder.

I look at your dog's attention, reinforcement, freedom, distractions and the habits that happen before you ever call them back. Reliable recall is built through repetition and good decisions, not one magic command.

What we'll work on

That may include engagement with you outdoors, building value into returning, using distance sensibly, timing your recall, rewarding the right decisions and preventing repeated opportunities to ignore you while the behaviour is being rebuilt.

Training that fits normal life

I want you to leave understanding how to practise recall during ordinary walks and daily routines. The goal is to give you a system you can keep using, rather than something that only works while a trainer is standing beside you.
