Dog reactivity training in Warrington

If walks have become a battle because your dog barks, lunges, fixates or becomes over-aroused around other dogs, people or traffic, we'll work on the behaviour where it actually happens.

Reactivity isn't one single problem.

I want to see what sets your dog off, what happens immediately beforehand, how you respond and what your dog has learned from that pattern. Then we can make practical changes rather than relying on a generic exercise.

What we'll work on

Depending on your dog, that can include attention around triggers, lead handling, creating workable distance, reducing unnecessary arousal, improving communication and giving you a clear plan for future walks.

Training in the real world

My intensive sessions take place at your home and in your local environment where appropriate. I demonstrate what I'm seeing and what I'd change, then coach you through it so you can continue after I leave.

The 1-to-1 intensive is £99 for up to two hours. You can choose an available appointment online and no online payment is required.
