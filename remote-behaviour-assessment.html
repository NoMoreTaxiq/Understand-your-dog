What you can expect

More than a few generic tips.

The point of the assessment is to help you understand what you're actually seeing and what to do next. I break the behaviour down, explain what matters, and turn that into practical training you can use in real life.

That can include changes to management, communication, boundaries, reinforcement and specific exercises depending on the dog and the behaviour shown in your footage.

Case Study: Resource Guarding & Food Aggression

A video can reveal far more than the obvious growl or snap. The assessment looks at the sequence leading up to it: posture, freezing, eye movement, tension, positioning, triggers and the human response.

From there, the training plan focuses on changing the pattern safely and practically rather than simply reacting to the final behaviour.
