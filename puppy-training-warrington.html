1-to-1 puppy training in Warrington

Puppy training isn't just sit and paw. It's teaching a young dog how everyday life works before jumping, pulling, ignoring you and charging through every doorway become established habits.

Start with the things you'll use every day.

Attention, recall, boundaries, calmness, lead walking, handling and basic obedience can all be built into normal routines. The whole household should understand the same rules so the puppy gets a consistent message.

What can we cover?

Recall, loose-lead walking, sit and stay, leave it, jumping up, puppy biting, toilet-training routines, door and threshold manners, over-excitement, settling, crate routines and the specific problems you're dealing with at home.

Why 1-to-1?

We can focus on your puppy, your household and the situations that matter to you instead of following a fixed class syllabus. I show you what to practise and how to make training part of daily life.
