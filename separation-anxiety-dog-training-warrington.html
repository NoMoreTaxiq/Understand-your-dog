Help for dogs struggling when left alone in Warrington

If leaving the house means barking, pacing, destruction, distress or a dog that cannot settle without you, the first job is understanding exactly what is happening rather than guessing.

Build independence rather than simply testing how long the dog can cope.

We look at the dog's routine, dependence on people, behaviour around departures and arrivals, ability to settle, environmental triggers and what happens as you prepare to leave. From there we build a practical plan around the individual dog.

What we can work on

Difficulty settling alone, following you constantly around the house, barking or vocalising after departure, destructive behaviour, over-arousal around leaving cues and building calmer everyday independence.

1-to-1 training at your home — £99
